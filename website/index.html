Privacy, for real

Klynto is local-first — and so is this website. There is no Klynto server, no account, and nothing to opt out of.

  • All analysis is local. Everything runs in your browser; results never leave your machine.
  • Per-site permissions. Klynto asks before inspecting a domain — never “read all your data on all websites” — and you can revoke any time.
  • Observation only. Klynto never modifies requests, never injects scripts, never phones home.
  • This website collects nothing. No cookies, no analytics, no trackers, no JavaScript.
